## Full Description (4000 characters max)

VoxCita is a private, local-first voice thinking companion for developers, community organisers, speakers, researchers and independent professionals.

Record meetings, talks, interviews or personal reflections. VoxCita converts your spoken words into timestamped transcripts, summaries, decisions, action items, questions and ideas. Every generated insight links to the precise transcript segments and audio timestamps that support it.

**Every insight has a source.**

- Tap a citation to hear the original audio (Source Tap)
- Claims without valid sources are marked unverified
- The system abstains when evidence is insufficient
- The distinction between AI-generated, user-corrected and user-verified information is preserved

**Privacy first.**

- All processing happens locally on your device
- No telemetry, no cloud required, no advertising
- Audio files never leave your device
- Biometric lock for app access
- Encrypted archive export and restoration

**Key features:**

- Resilient audio recording with real-time waveform
- Timestamped transcription with segment-level editing
- Transcript correction with revision history
- Cited insights: summaries, decisions, actions, questions, ideas
- Citation validation and evidence timeline
- Trustworthy Ask: ask questions about your recordings with cited answers
- Confidence scoring and abstention when evidence is insufficient
- Library with full-text search, collections and tags
- Privacy-preserving analytics (all local, nothing transmitted)
- User feedback on insights
- Light, dark and high-contrast themes

VoxCita does not contain hidden recording functionality. VoxCita does not sell recording data. VoxCita does not contain advertising. Explicit consent is required before sending any material to a network provider.
